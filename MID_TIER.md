# Broader Browser qualification

No mid-tier cases are retained, implemented or executed. This is an obligation
list, not a passing suite. `test-tiers.json` records named unavailable routes
independently of a top-tier pass.

Foundation top-tier evidence does not establish successful native host start,
LaunchServices/helper provenance, lifetime custody, full close/flush/clean marker,
engine reader/epoch compatibility or actual Electron session persistence. It
does not establish positive controlling-TTY deletion, all filesystem races,
capacity, cross-UID peers or power-loss durability. Distinct consequential future
cases need individual admission and tier review; do not generate every hand-over
obligation as a test or silently turn these gaps into new top-tier cases.

Native/page/control/Driver, signing/release/install/update and real account/model
journeys remain with their qualified services and separately bounded scope.
Mid-tier execution requires explicit owner scope. Missing native/GUI evidence is
unavailable, never an exclusion pass from a failed tool.

No low-tier bodies existed; none were invented or silently re-enabled.
